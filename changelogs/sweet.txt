This is Android 16 with August 2026 security patches

Notes:
==============================
- LineageOS-based source. Signed build.
- Clean flash mandatory when your build is from Evolution X 8 or lower
- MIGHT need a clean flash from 11.5.3

Known Bugs:
==============================
- If you update from 11.5.3 to 11.10, you might get a blurred screen on boot. In that case you need to format data

Device changes:
==============================
- Update to Evolution X 11.10
- Implemented MIUI camera
- Added EROFS for vendor and odm partition
