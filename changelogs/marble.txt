This is Android 17 QPR0 with November 2026 security patches

Notes:
==============================
- Final stock global firmware is pre-included. You may flash the final firmware for your device's variant/region.
- First install of this update will replace your current recovery to stock evo recovery.
- You may keep the new stock evo recovery or flash OrangeFox-R12.0_20261115_6.6-Test-marble.img after the ROM or via fastboot.
- Always dirty flash at your own risk. If your device bootloops, revert to an older build that boots.
- KSU next is now pre-included. Just install the latest KSU next manager app.
- Includes POCO camera and Dolby Atmos and Dolby Vision (still WIP).
- Credits to Arian16k, GR, Chaitanya, Adrianyyyy.
