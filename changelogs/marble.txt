This is Android 16 QPR1 with January 2026 security patches

Notes:
==============================
- Install the latest HyperOS 2 FW according to your region (https://xmfirmwareupdater.com/firmware/marble)
- Use OFRP-R11.1_7-Beta-marble.zip as recovery.
- Always dirty flash at your own risk. If your device bootloops, revert to an older build that boots.
- Install this (https://sourceforge.net/projects/evolution-x/files/marble/16/Evo-11.x-5.10.245-KSU-Next-v1.1.1-susfs-1.5.12.zip/download) for root.
- Includes POCO camera and Dolby Atmos.
